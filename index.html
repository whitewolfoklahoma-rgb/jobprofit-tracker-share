❓ Help & support

ProfitFlag v1.1

New to ProfitFlag, or stuck on something? Start with these short how-tos — they cover the jobs the app is used for every day.

Add a job

1) Open the Jobs tab and tap + New Job. 2) Fill in the customer name and the job title or address — those two are required — then add the status, quoted amount, start and due dates, and any notes if you have them. 3) Tap Save job. The job appears in the Jobs list and on the Dashboard.

Log invoices, payments & expenses (costs & materials)

1) Open a job from the Jobs list or the Dashboard to see its detail page. 2) Tap + Invoice, + Payment, or + Expense. 3) Enter the amount and date, add a note, and for an expense pick a category (Labor, Materials, Fuel, Equipment, Disposal / dump fees, Subcontractors, or Other), then tap Save. Every entry shows in that job’s ledger, and you can remove one with its ✕ button. If a payment brings an invoiced job’s balance to $0, the job flips to Paid on its own.

Enter by voice (dictation)

Tap the 🎤 button beside any name, note, or amount field and say the entry out loud — the words land in the field exactly as if you typed them, and nothing is saved until you tap Save. For amounts, plain spoken numbers work: say “four hundred twenty dollars” and the field gets 420. Voice entry needs a browser or device with speech recognition (it works in the Google Play edition and on the hosted web version). Where voice isn’t available, the mic button shows a short note and typing works as always.

Read your profit

The Dashboard shows totals across all jobs: quoted pipeline, invoiced, collected, outstanding, total expenses, and total profit. Open any job for its own figures: quoted, invoiced, collected, balance due, expenses, profit, and margin. Profit is the money you have collected minus your expenses, and margin is that profit as a share of what you collected. A job whose expenses pass its quote is flagged LOSING $, and a job past due with a balance still owed is flagged OVERDUE.

Print a statement for a customer

1) Open the job. 2) Tap 🖨 Print statement / invoice at the top of the job detail page. The statement shows your company name and trade (from Settings), the job, and its charges and payments only — your internal expenses and costs never appear on it.

Back up & restore your data

Go to Settings → Data and tap Export backup (JSON) — that JSON file is the restorable backup. To bring it back on this or a new device, tap Import backup in the same place and pick that file; importing replaces the jobs and settings currently in the app, so the app asks you to confirm first. The CSV exports are for spreadsheets and your accountant — they cannot be restored into the app.

Your data stays on this device — ProfitFlag stores everything only in this browser on this device, with no account and no cloud. Back it up with Settings → Data → Export backup (JSON); that file is also how you move your records to a new device.
